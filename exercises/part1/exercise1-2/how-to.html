How to Register for an Exam

This guide explains the main steps a student can follow to register for an examination.

Before You Register

Before registering, check the examination date, registration deadline, and instructions provided by your school or examination body.

Check the Exam Date

Find out when the examination will take place so that you can register before the deadline.

Confirm the Requirements

Ask your school or examination body which forms, identification documents, and fees are required.

What to Bring

Prepare the documents and information required for registration before visiting the registration office.

At the Registration Office

Follow the official instructions, complete the registration form carefully, and submit the required documents.

After Registration

Keep your registration receipt or confirmation and check that your examination details are correct.
